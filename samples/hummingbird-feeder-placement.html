Best Place to Put Hummingbird Feeders

Are you one of the bird enthusiasts who wish that Hummingbirds visit your home? Well, reaching your goal is not as hard as memorizing the scientific names of their whole species. But without the proper knowledge, you can only expect them to pass through your home and not spend time there. In some cases, an incorrect setup of Hummingbird feeders can result in either hurting the birds or to its owners. So before giving it a go, make sure to know more about bird feeder installation below to avoid gaining harm than its benefits.

Now the question is: “How can we make these birds linger on your lawn?”. Like any other animal, Hummingbirds have their own set of needs that affect their behavior and activities. Primarily, they go around to either look for nutrition, shelter, and mate to reproduce. They do this because the three are the essential things they need to survive and thrive in their species. Using one of these, you can lure Hummingbirds to your home and see their colorful features rather than lucky encounters only.

Attracting hummingbirds can be easier with this in mind; however, they are one of the migratory bird species. This means, hummingbirds do not stay too long in an area so baiting them using mating can be a waste. Making a shelter for them can also be a waste in some areas since they can live in any tree within their reach. That makes it nutrition as the best luring material to persuade. But it does not end there, the next important thing is what food and where should it be placed to effectively draw them.

In terms of “what” food, commercialized bird feeders became the medium for migrating hummingbirds and encourage them to stay. They contain sweetened water solution or nectar which is one of the main components of the birds’ diet.

Now that you know what type of food to attract hummingbirds with, you should also know where to install them. This is because, without proper placement, the nectar will go to waste since the birds will not entertain them. There can never be any definite location to set up bird feeders but here are the things to consider to lure them.

Provide Shade

There are two main reasons why shade is essential for hummingbird feeder placement. One is due to the negative influence of excessive heat during the day or rain. Even hummingbirds suffer when exposed to a high temperature around noon time. Another reason is to avoid spoilage of the sweetened water because of the weather.

One way to solve these problems is to install bird feeders near your house, trees, or high bushes. Make sure that they will block the sun during the right time of the day. This can also attract aggressive hummingbirds since they can easily defend their feeders due to nearby shelters.

2. Ensure Protection

Another thing to consider is safety. This applies to both the hummingbirds and the feeder owners. It is natural for small birds to have predators like rodents, mammals, and even other birds. That is why you should place your hummingbird feeders away from your household pets and potential hazards to prevent harm. No bird will go near a feeder that is infested with dangerous animals so keep the place safe for them. You should also put the feeders away from windows since it is common that they would not notice the glass. This could result in injuries when they try to go through closed windows unknowingly.

Maintenance for your feeders is also a must since the sweet solution is prone to spoilage. These can also become dirty because of exposure from natural elements like molds and bacteria. For this reason, it is a must to clean them regularly whenever possible to easily lure in hummingbirds. But since the locations of feeders can be difficult to reach, you should make sure that your safety when its time to retrieve them.

3. Multiple Hummingbird Feeder Placement

Hummingbirds are similar to humans since they can have different preferences when it comes to bird feeders. Some may like to be near a tree so that their shelter will be near their feeders. It can be far away from your house since they dislike the threat of being too close to humans. While others may want their food source to be away from others to ensure their ownership of the feeder. Due to these reasons, you should try to set up as many hummingbird feeders as possible at different locations.

4. Promote Diversity On Your Garden

You may consider enhancing your yard to attract hummingbirds more. A lawn with flowers will draw them more since they have bright colors and they contain nectar besides your feeders. Avoiding the use of pesticides can also help charm these birds since they also feed on small insects like spiders. This is because small insects are their source of protein.

5. Make Sure They Are Visible To You

In case you are enthusiastic to see hummingbirds, you should also take your viewing points into account. Place bird feeders clearly seen from your windows, balconies, and porches to see these colorful birds.

Conclusion

Common with all animals and even humans, hummingbirds need food, shelter, and mate to thrive. With this, it is great to set up bird feeders on your lawn because they provide more food for hummingbirds. This can also help their species thrive due to the decreasing amount of sources of nectar on their habitats. In return for the bird feeder owners, the birds are remarkable because of their colorful feathers. This can be very rewarding for bird enthusiasts and even others because the sight of them can be relaxing. However, you will need to exert some effort so that hummingbirds would visit your lawn.

Aside from preparing nectar, which is one of the primary nutrition sources of hummingbirds, and bird feeders, proper placement of these materials is also necessary. The bird feeders should not endanger hummingbirds or be exposed to bad weather and extreme temperatures. Some of these birds also have their preferences like isolated bird feeders and others. Improving your lawn helps to attract them like putting on flowers and letting insects linger there. But if you really want to see them, you should make it visible to you.

Where should hummingbird feeders be placed in sun or shade?

It is the best hummingbird feeder placement in shaded areas during the height of noon. This is to avoid spoilage of hummingbird feeders and exposing the birds to too much heat. But, do not set the feeders on too much shade that it will not be visible to you and the hummingbirds. So this does not mean that the hummingbird feeders should be always under the shade.

How far apart should you hang your hummingbird feeders?

When having multiple hummingbird feeder placements, it is necessary to put them away from each other. Since hummingbirds are known to be territorial around their feeders, it will not be good to put them too near. There are birds that are more aggressive than the others so to give chance to the lesser ones, attach them around 15 to 20 feet apart.

How do you hang a hummingbird feeder?

Different products of hummingbird feeders may have either a hook or a string. If the feeder has a hook, attach it to any stable rods or branches. You can choose wherever hummingbirds are likely to feed. Otherwise, install any firm hook rod on any good location and hang the feeder on it.

What time of day do hummingbirds come to feeders?

Hummingbirds prefer early morning and late afternoon times to feed on nectar. This is because of more moderate temperatures during these hours. So when wishing to watch them around hummingbird feeders, try to look for them both in the morning and afternoon.

Why do hummingbirds chase each other away from the feeder?

These birds are aware of the scarce flowers so they tend to protect their source of nutrition. But they also apply their territorial behavior with the feeders since it is the same with flowers that serve them nectar. Another thing that makes hummingbirds aggressive is when male birds try to impress their female mate.

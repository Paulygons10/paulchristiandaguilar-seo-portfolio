## Content Approach

My approach to SEO content focuses on balancing search optimization with useful, readable content.

### 1. Understand the Topic

Identify the purpose of the page and the information the intended audience needs.

### 2. Identify Target Keywords

Research and organize the primary and supporting keywords relevant to the content.

### 3. Build the Content Structure

Organize headings, sections, and supporting information around the target topic and search intent.

### 4. Write for the Reader

Create clear and useful content while naturally incorporating the target keywords.

### 5. Optimize the Page

Review keyword placement, headings, readability, grammar, and overall on-page SEO elements.

### 6. Refine the Content

Edit the final content to improve clarity, consistency, and readability while maintaining the intended SEO direction.
